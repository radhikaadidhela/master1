# Salesforce Clouds and Product Topics

## Sales Cloud
Leads, Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, products/price books, forecasting, sales automation, reports/dashboards; integration and automation patterns.

## Service Cloud
Cases, Email-to-Case/Web-to-Case, Service Console, Knowledge, Omni-Channel, entitlements/milestones concepts, CTI/NICE CXone, automation and escalation.

## Experience Cloud
External portals/sites, authenticated users, sharing sets/roles/sharing, LWC experiences, security, performance, server-side authorization.

## Financial Services Cloud (FSC)
Use careful positioning: understanding/preparation plus financial-services-related Salesforce experience, not deep multi-year FSC production unless proven.
Core model: Person Account = individual; Business Account = company; Financial Account = product/account; Financial Account Role links people/entities; Holdings; Relationships/Groups/Household; Goals; Life Events; Action Plans.
Person Account = WHO; Financial Account = WHAT.
Core banking remains authoritative for transactional balances/financial execution.

## Health Cloud
Interview preparation should cover patient/member 360, care teams, care plans, clinical/service context, consent/security/integration patterns. Do not claim production Health Cloud unless resume supports it.

## CPQ / Revenue Cloud / RCA
CPQ extends opportunity into product configuration, pricing and quoting, then feeds contract/order/subscription/billing processes/integrations.
Opportunity Product represents product associated with opportunity; Quote Line represents product on CPQ quote.
Revenue Cloud/RCA knowledge should be labeled appropriately where conceptual.

## Marketing Cloud
Quant Systems resume supports Journey Builder, Email Studio, Content Builder, Data Extensions, Marketing Cloud Connect, responsive HTML/CSS email and reusable templates.
Automation Studio = scheduled data/audience processing; Journey Builder = timed/orchestrated customer journey. Respect subscriber status, publication preferences, consent/suppression/privacy. Unsubscribe does not automatically delete all customer data.

## CRM Analytics / reporting
Prepare CRMA concepts, datasets/recipes/dataflows, dashboards, security, Data 360 integration, and comparison with Power BI where requested. Avoid claiming unsupported deep production ownership.
